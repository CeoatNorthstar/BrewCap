## Installation

### Step 1: Install BrewCap
Open Terminal and run:

```
brew tap CeoatNorthstar/brewcap
brew install --cask brewcap
```

### Step 2: Activate Your License
1. Open BrewCap (click the battery icon in your menu bar)
2. Click "Open BrewCap" or use the main window
3. Go to **Settings** in the sidebar
4. Click **"Activate License"**
5. Paste your license key
6. Click **"Activate"**
